Shvasa One Referral System

Overview

The system at a glance

Everyone shares the same personal link. The reward comes after the friend's 5-day money-back period.

Key terms

Words used in this page

Each term has one meaning. The rules and paths below use these words only in this sense.

TermMeaning
ReferrerAny user who shares their personal link. Paid, free-trial and lapsed users can all be referrers.
FriendA person who opens a referrer's link and buys Shvasa One.
Personal linkThe one link every user has. It opens the Shvasa One landing page and never expires.
Shvasa OneThe self-checkout plan, sold as a 3-month plan and an annual plan.
Qualifying purchaseA Shvasa One 3-month or annual purchase made through a link.
Money-back periodThe 5 days after a Shvasa One purchase in which it can be refunded. Refund terms: within 5 days of purchase, at least 2 classes attended, Rs 99 administration charge deducted.
Day 6The first day after the money-back period. Rewards are given on this day. Day 1 is the day of purchase [to confirm].
RewardBonus Shvasa One time. 2 weeks for a 3-month plan, 1 month (30 days) for an annual plan. Friend and referrer get the same amount.
Unclaimed rewardA reward sitting in the referrer's account. It does nothing until the referrer taps Claim.
Paid memberA user with an active paid subscription of any kind.
Lapsed userA user whose subscription has expired. There is no wait period before they can be a friend.
Primary subscriptionThe one subscription that holds a user's credits. A private plan also holds the user's group class credits.
Shvasa One creditsThe class credits that come with Shvasa One. Credits decide which classes a user can book. Other features are open to all.
The rules

Every rule, numbered

Rules are numbered R1 to R19. The paths, examples and requirements below point back to them.

R2The Refer and earn entry point is shown on the website (top left corner), in the app (Progress page) and on the mobile website. It is not shown to users outside India.

R8The plan the friend buys sets the reward: 3-month = 2 weeks, annual = 1 month (30 days). The friend and the referrer get the same amount. Every referral earns the same. There is no ladder and no cap.

R11The referrer's reward is not automatic. On day 6 it is added to their account as an unclaimed reward. They claim it anytime. Each reward has its own Claim button. There is no bulk claim.

Every path

What happens in each case

Ten paths cover everything a referrer or a friend can do. Each lists the steps in order and what each person ends up with.

Path 1 · Referrer shares

A user shares their link

  1. The user opens Refer and earn (R2) or sees a share prompt.
  2. They copy or share their personal link (R3).

Friend: nothing yet.

Referrer: nothing yet. The link is reusable and never expires.

Path 2 · Main path

An eligible friend buys and keeps the plan

  1. The friend opens the link and sees the landing page with "[Name] referred you" (R4).
  2. The friend enters their mobile number. The system finds they are new, on a free trial or lapsed, so they are eligible (R5).
  3. The friend buys a Shvasa One 3-month or annual plan. The purchase summary shows their bonus. The purchase is credited to the link used at payment (R6, R7).
  4. The 5-day money-back period runs. The friend does not refund.
  5. On day 6 both rewards are given (R9).

Friend: plan extended by 2 weeks (3-month) or 1 month (annual), with Shvasa One credits added (R10).

Referrer: one unclaimed reward of the same size in their account, claimable anytime (R11). Notified on WhatsApp (R17).

Path 3 · Not eligible

A paid member opens a link

  1. They open the link and see the same landing page as everyone (R4).
  2. They enter their mobile number. The system finds an active paid subscription.
  3. They see: "Sorry, looks like you already have an active subscription with us." (R5)

Friend: not referred. No bonus.

Referrer: nothing.

Path 4 · Refund

The friend refunds inside the 5 days

  1. The friend buys through a link.
  2. Within 5 days they ask for a refund, under the refund terms.
  3. Day 6 never gives a reward (R14).

Friend: no bonus.

Referrer: no reward.

Path 5 · No purchase

A friend opens the link and does not buy

  1. The friend opens the link and leaves.
  2. Nothing expires: not the link, not the offer (R3, R18).
  3. If they buy later through the same link, path 2 starts from the purchase.

Friend: no new link is needed.

Referrer: nothing until a purchase happens.

Path 6 · Two links

A friend opens links from two users

  1. The friend opens user A's link, then user B's link.
  2. They pay using B's link.
  3. The purchase is credited to B (R7).

Friend: gets the bonus once, as in path 2.

Referrer A: nothing. Referrer B: one reward.

Path 7 · Several friends

Many friends buy close together

  1. Each friend's purchase starts its own 5-day wait (R13).
  2. Each purchase reaches its own day 6 and gives its own reward.
  3. Rewards appear in the referrer's account one after another.

Friend: as in path 2, each on their own day 6.

Referrer: one unclaimed reward per qualifying friend. Each is claimed separately.

Path 8 · Claiming

The referrer claims a reward

  1. The referrer opens their referral page and sees their unclaimed rewards (R11).
  2. They tap Claim on one reward. One tap claims one reward only.
  3. The system checks their status and applies the reward as shown in the Claiming chart below (R12).

Active subscription (any plan): the plan stays the same. Its end date moves out by the reward (also in the third-party app). Shvasa One credits are added. No credits of its own type (1:1, unlimited and so on) are added. It keeps renewing as the original plan, not as Shvasa One. If the user has several subscriptions, the primary one is extended.

Expired subscription: a Shvasa One subscription is activated. It is a one-time plan and does not renew. [Length: equal to the reward, to confirm]

Active free trial: the free trial is cancelled and a Shvasa One subscription is activated. Remaining trial credits expire because credits are not transferable. It is a one-time plan and does not renew. [Length: equal to the reward, to confirm]

Path 9 · Account removed

The referrer's account is deleted or blocked

  1. The referrer has unclaimed rewards.
  2. Their account is deleted or blocked before they claim.
  3. The unclaimed rewards are removed (R16).

Friend: keeps their bonus.

Referrer: loses the unclaimed rewards.

Path 10 · Dispute

A chargeback or dispute after day 6

  1. Day 6 has passed and rewards were given.
  2. The friend raises a chargeback or dispute.
  3. Nothing is removed automatically. The team settles it case by case (R15).

Friend and referrer: rewards stay unless the team decides otherwise.

Rule R12, shown

What happens when a reward is claimed

The system checks the claimer's status at the moment of the tap. One tap claims one reward.

Stacking, by type of user Proposed

UserFirst claimEach later claim
Paid (active subscription)The end date moves out by the reward.The end date moves out again by that reward. Two rewards of 1 month and 2 weeks move it out by 1 month and 2 weeks in total.
Lapsed (expired)A one-time Shvasa One plan is activated.The user now has an active Shvasa One plan, so the claim extends it, as for a paid user. It still does not renew.
Free trialThe trial is cancelled and a one-time Shvasa One plan is activated.Extends that plan, as for a paid user. It still does not renew.

Status is checked at each tap. If a one-time plan has ended before the next claim, the user is lapsed again and that claim activates a new one-time plan.

Worked examples

The rules in use

Four examples with the exact outcome. Days are counted from the purchase day, which is day 1.

Example 1 · Referrer has a private plan

A new friend buys the annual plan on day 1 through the link of a user on a private 10-pack, 2-month plan. No refund.

Day 6, friend: annual plan extended by 1 month, Shvasa One credits added.

Day 6, referrer: one unclaimed reward of 1 month.

When claimed: the private plan keeps its 10 1:1 credits, runs 1 month longer, gets Shvasa One credits, and keeps renewing as a private plan.

Example 2 · Refund

A friend buys the 3-month plan on day 1 and refunds on day 4.

Day 6, friend: no bonus.

Day 6, referrer: no reward.

Example 3 · Referrer's plan has expired

A friend buys the 3-month plan on day 1 through the link of a user whose subscription expired. No refund.

Day 6, friend: plan extended by 2 weeks.

Day 6, referrer: one unclaimed reward of 2 weeks.

When claimed: a one-time Shvasa One subscription is activated. It does not renew.

Example 4 · Referrer is on a free trial

A friend buys the annual plan on day 1 through the link of a user on an active free trial. No refund.

Day 6, friend: annual plan extended by 1 month.

Day 6, referrer: one unclaimed reward of 1 month.

When claimed: the free trial is cancelled, remaining trial credits expire, and a one-time Shvasa One subscription is activated. It does not renew.

Edge cases

Other cases and known risks

Cases not covered by a path, and what is still open.

SituationHow it is handled
Link shared publicly or misusedLinks are meant to be shared publicly. A link can still be switched off if misused.
Paused plans, YTT, private packagesNot an issue. Every plan has a clear end date, and paused days are finite and stated by the user.
Credits left overCredits are not transferable and expire when the subscription does.
Family and Friends, Duo, couplesEach member needs their own account. Payments are made separately but can use the same payment method every time.
Support tool to grant or remove rewardsNot required now.

Known risk. Ideally a friend whose mobile number, payment ID or device matches the referrer's would be invalid, but this is not built now. Because family members can legitimately share a payment method, a match alone is not proof of abuse.

To confirm. (1) Day counting: purchase day is day 1 and the money-back period is days 1 to 5. (2) The length of the one-time Shvasa One plan for expired and free-trial users. (3) How the renewal date moves when an auto-renewing subscription is extended. (4) Whether a paid member who is stopped by the mobile check can still buy Shvasa One at the normal price. (5) How a link is switched off, and whether the off button covers the whole system or single links. (6) The WhatsApp events to notify. (7) Event logging (FR-14), not yet raised with the CTO. (8) Stacking (R19): whether any limit is wanted, since many referrals could add up to a long free period.
